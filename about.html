About Converter Genie

Simple tools to make everyday conversions easier.

Who We Are

Welcome to Converter Genie! Our goal is to make common file conversions and everyday calculations simple and convenient.

Our Tools

Converter Genie is designed to provide useful tools, including:

  • Image conversion between supported image formats.
  • PDF tools for combining PDF documents.
  • Unit conversion for length, weight and temperature.

Privacy Matters

Our image and unit converters work directly in your browser. The PDF merge tool also processes selected documents in your browser using a JavaScript library loaded from an external CDN. We aim to avoid uploading your files to our own server.

Please note that external services, hosting providers and third-party libraries may have their own technical practices. Read our Privacy Policy for more information.

Our Mission

We want to build a simple, accessible and helpful collection of online tools that people can use on different devices.
