Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to Converter Genie.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Converter Genie handles information when you visit and use our website.

2. Files and Conversions

Our image converter processes images in your browser using browser-based features. Our unit converter performs calculations in your browser. Our PDF merge tool processes selected PDF files in your browser using the pdf-lib JavaScript library.

These tools are designed to process your selected files locally rather than upload them to our own server. However, the PDF library is loaded from a third-party content delivery network (CDN), which may receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address when your browser requests the library.

3. Information We Receive

We do not intentionally ask you to provide personal information to use the basic conversion tools. If you contact us by email, we will receive the information you include in your message, such as your email address and the contents of your inquiry.

4. Cookies and Analytics

This starter version of Converter Genie does not intentionally set its own analytics or advertising cookies. Hosting providers or third-party services may process technical information as part of delivering the website.

If analytics, advertising, or other third-party services are added in the future, this policy should be updated to describe the services and their data practices.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website may rely on services such as website hosting and external JavaScript libraries. These services have their own privacy policies and practices.

6. Data Security

We aim to keep the website simple and minimize unnecessary collection of personal information. No website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Our tools are general-purpose utilities and are not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our website or services change. Updates will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, visit our Contact page and use the real email address listed there once it has been added.
